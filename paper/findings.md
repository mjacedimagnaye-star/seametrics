## 1. Executive Overview of Findings

This document compiles the exhaustive empirical findings generated by the SeaMetrics analytical engine across six calendar years (**2019, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026**). All metrics are directly calculated from official Philippine Ports Authority (PPA) statistical reports and standardized using the automated SeaMetrics data pipeline.

### Core Key Performance Indicators (At-a-Glance)
* **Historical Multi-Year Horizon:** 6 Years (2019 pre-pandemic commodity baseline; 2022–2026 continuous quarterly and monthly series).
* **Included Geographic Scope:** 108 verified Luzon port facilities and operational berths across 10 Port Management Offices (PMOs). All Visayas and Mindanao ports are strictly excluded.
* **Peak 9-Month Passenger Throughput:** **17,013,983 passengers** in 2025 (Q1–Q3), stabilizing at **16,784,830 passengers** in 2026 (Q1–Q3).
* **Peak Full-Year Passenger Volume:** **22,051,885 passengers** (Full Year 2025).
* **Multi-Year Passenger Growth (2022 to 2026 Q1–Q3):** **+36.25%** overall expansion.
* **Corridor Concentration:** The Southwestern nautical corridor (PMO Batangas + PMO Mindoro) accounts for **53.79%** of all Luzon passenger traffic and **57.14%** of all RoRo vehicle traffic in 2026 Q1–Q3.
* **Busiest Port in Luzon:** **Base Port of Batangas (BP Batangas)** across all three metrics (3,780,022 pax; 683,803 RoRo vehicles; 25,695 ship calls in 2026 Q1–Q3).
* **Seasonality Extreme:** Traffic peaks in **May (Seasonality Index: 164.94)** and contracts to its annual trough in **September (Seasonality Index: 65.61)** during the Southwest Monsoon (*Habagat*).
* **RoRo Fleet Composition:** Dominated by commercial heavy freight (**Type 4: 40.20%**) and passenger cars (**Type 2: 33.10%**).
* **Best Forecasting Model:** The **Naive Seasonal Benchmark ($Y_{t-4}$)** achieved **4.62% MAPE** on held-out 2026 actuals for Passenger Traffic.

## 5. Batangas Port vs. Calapan Port Corridor Analysis

### Mandatory Data Integrity Disclosure
Port-wide statistics represent overall terminal throughput at **Batangas Port (`BP Batangas`)** and **Calapan Port (`BP Calapan`)** as regional gateway facilities. Because PPA records statistics by terminal facility rather than origin-destination ticket manifests, these figures represent gateway throughput. Batangas Port functions as mainland Luzon's premier inter-island hub, while Calapan Port serves as Oriental Mindoro's primary nautical gateway.

### Corridor Key Insights
* **Near-Perfect Directional Equilibrium:** The ratio of disembarking passengers to embarking passengers is almost exactly 1.00 at both facilities (net migration in 2026 Q1–Q3 was only -2,674 at Batangas and +2,177 at Calapan), proving that travel on this corridor is almost purely circulatory commuter and tourist traffic.
* **Corridor Volume Concentration:** Combined, Batangas Port and Calapan Port handle **6,319,409 passengers (61.9% of all Southern Luzon base port passengers)** and **1,127,071 RoRo vehicles (54.5% of Southern Luzon base port RoRo volume)**.

## 7. RoRo Fleet Classification Breakdown

Across the 2,676,093 RoRo vehicles recorded in Luzon during 2026 Q1–Q3:
1. **Type 4 (Heavy Multi-Axle Freight Trucks & Provincial Buses):** **1,075,789 vehicles (40.20%)**.
2. **Type 2 (Passenger Cars, SUVs, AUVs, Jeeps $\le$ 16 pax):** **885,786 vehicles (33.10%)**.
3. **Type 1 (Motorcycles, Tricycles, Scooters):** **428,175 vehicles (16.00%)**.
4. **Type 3 (Light Commercial Delivery Vans, Pickups, PUJs):** **286,343 vehicles (10.70%)**.

**Significance:** More than 50% of rolling vehicles (Types 3 and 4) are commercial delivery freight units transporting agricultural produce, meat, construction supplies, and merchandise between mainland Luzon and island provinces.
